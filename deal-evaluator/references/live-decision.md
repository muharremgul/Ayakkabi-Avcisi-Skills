# Live deal rules

Base the decision on exact-size verified offers. Separate item price, shipping, coupons, and membership/card conditions. Do not calculate savings against an unverified list price.

Assess:

- fit for the user
- landed price versus budget and verified competitors
- seller identity and authorization
- return, warranty, condition, and delivery
- price history only when a comparable prior snapshot exists

A cheap marketplace offer with unclear seller or returns may rank below a slightly more expensive authorized offer.
