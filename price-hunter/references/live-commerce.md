# Turkey live commerce protocol

## Coverage groups

Search a broad, rotating set rather than claiming every store:

- official brand stores and authorized dealers
- running, trail, outdoor, sports, sneaker, and department stores
- Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Amazon.com.tr, N11, Pazarama, idefix, and other relevant marketplaces
- Akakçe, Cimri, Epey, Google Shopping, and comparable discovery services

Add newly discovered legitimate Turkish merchants. Preserve seller identity on marketplaces.

## Verification

Open the direct product page. Confirm exact model/variant and exact requested size. A size counts only when enabled/selectable; a safe reversible cart check may strengthen proof. Do not buy, submit payment, or bypass access controls.

Record price, unavoidable shipping, coupon requirements, seller, returns, check time, URL, and stock evidence. Stale snippets and aggregators stay in a discovery-only section.

## Safety language

Use “doğrulandı” only for live direct evidence. Use “listelendi”, “belirsiz”, “erişim engelli”, or “stokta yok” for all other states.
