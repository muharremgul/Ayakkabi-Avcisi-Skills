# Current research protocol

## Evidence ladder

Use multiple independent evidence classes:

1. official brand page for identity and specifications
2. independent lab or technical reviews, prioritizing applicable sources such as RunRepeat, RoadTrailRun, Doctors of Running, OutdoorGearLab, iRunFar, Believe in the Run, and specialist local publications
3. long-term field reviews
4. Reddit threads and YouTube transcripts/comments when directly accessible
5. verified-purchase retailer feedback
6. search snippets and aggregators for discovery only

RunRepeat or an equivalent independent lab source should be checked when it covers the exact model. Do not treat any single publication as mandatory if the model is absent or its test method is unsuitable.

Use Agent Reach for Reddit and YouTube when available, but do not make either platform a single point of failure. Treat its `doctor` result as a health hint: a connected OpenCLI bridge still requires a live read-only Reddit smoke test, and a YouTube configuration warning may be superseded by a successful live extraction. An inaccessible social channel lowers community-evidence confidence; it does not block an analysis supported by independent technical and long-term sources.

## Freshness

- Product specifications may use stable official pages.
- Current stock and price require a direct page opened during the current run.
- Reviews must include publication dates or approximate age when available.
- Report the Europe/Istanbul verification time for live offers.

## Community evidence

Separate creator/reviewer claims from user comments. Report sample counts. “Recurring” requires multiple independent observations; “consensus” requires broad, cross-source agreement.

## Commerce proof

An aggregator, search result, cached snippet, structured-data badge, or general “in stock” label does not prove the requested size. Exact size must be visibly selectable or confirmed by a live stock signal.

## Conflicts

When sources disagree, preserve both positions and explain likely causes such as runner weight, pace, terrain, fit, production variant, or test duration.
