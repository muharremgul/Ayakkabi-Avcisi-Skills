# Current recommendation rules

Build the performance shortlist first, then verify current price and requested-size availability.

Use official specifications plus independent sources such as RunRepeat, RoadTrailRun, Doctors of Running, OutdoorGearLab, iRunFar, Believe in the Run, specialist local reviews, long-term field reviews, Reddit, YouTube transcripts/comments, and verified-purchase feedback when applicable and accessible.

No single source controls the verdict. Keep laboratory measurements, reviewer opinion, community reports, and commerce evidence separate.

A high performance-fit score with no live exact-size stock is a model recommendation, not an available purchase.
